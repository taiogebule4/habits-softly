# Habits Softly Support

Email **habitssoftly@gmail.com**. We read everything and usually reply within two working days.

## Delete your account

Everything about your account is removed at once, on our servers and on your phone. It cannot be undone.

1. Open Habits Softly.
2. Open the **Manage** tab.
3. Under **Account**, tap **Delete account** and confirm.

Deleted: your habits, check-ins, challenges, streak freezes, settings, name, profile photo, sign-in details, push-notification registrations and cached AI summaries.

Can't sign in? Email **habitssoftly@gmail.com** from the address on the account, subject "Delete my account". We will delete it within 7 days and confirm by reply. We never need your password.

## Password reset

On the sign-in screen, tap **Forgot your password?** and enter your email. Open the email **on the phone you use Habits Softly on** and tap the button; it opens the app to set a new password. Each link works once and expires after an hour. If you opened it on a computer first, request a new one.

## Reminders not arriving

- Check the app's notification permission in your phone's settings.
- In the app, Manage → Reminders must be on, and the habit needs a reminder time.
- iPhone: Focus modes and Scheduled Summary can hold notifications back.
- Android: battery optimisation can delay them; allow the app to run in the background.

## Sign in with Apple or Google

If the button fails, try email and password with the same address; the account is the same. Google sign-in on Android requires Google Play services.

## Your data

Ask for a copy or a correction by email. Your habits are also visible in the app under History.
